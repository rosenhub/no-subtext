Great question! This is a really common dilemma and honestly, there's no one-size-fits-all answer. It kind of depends on your situation, your goals, and your comfort level with risk. Some people find that a HELOC gives them more flexibility, while others prefer the predictability of a refi. You might want to think about how long you plan to stay in the home, and it could be worth running the numbers with a professional. That said, if I had to lean one way, I'd probably say a HELOC might make sense if you don't need all the money at once, but again, it really comes down to what feels right for you. Hope this helps, and feel free to ask if you have any other questions!
